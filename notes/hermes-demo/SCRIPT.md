# xscapes for Hermes: demo script v1 (2026-11-10, for his notes)

60 s, 16:9 1920x1080, 30 fps. One real `xscapes hermes` session recorded in a
hidden terminal (124x44), every frame the real terminal; time compressed in
the edit, the scene's own motion kept at real speed. Captions lowercase, five
words or fewer. Sound: the product's own cues at their real moments over a
quiet bed, no voice. Pipeline: notes/trailer (Sept), extended for a recorded
session.
